The Formula

Crafted Perfection

Every Lueur gloss begins with an obsession for light. Our chemists spent three years perfecting a base that feels weightless yet performs like armor — a formula that moves with your lips instead of sitting on them. We infuse each batch with cold-pressed jojoba oil, vitamin E, and a micro-fine pearl complex sourced from sustainable studios in Grasse, France. The result is a gloss that catches light from every angle without a single grain of grit or a trace of stickiness. Inspired by candlelit dinners and golden-hour skin, Lueur was designed for the woman who wants her lips to look lit from within — not painted on. It's the difference between wearing gloss and glowing.
